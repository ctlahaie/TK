Methodology & notes
• Employee annual costs are the 2027 Employee Annual Costs for the selected employee group.
• Benefits are from the BCBSM Benefits-at-a-Glance documents: Value HSA Level 139/140 (effective 1/1/2027); Value HSA 2000 115/116, Value 1000 188, Enhanced HSA 2000 040/041 and Enhanced 500 008 (effective 1/1/2025). Confirm 2027 benefit levels for those four plans.
• Usage is spread evenly across the year and processed month by month. Medical costs assume one $150 office visit per $500 of medical cost (up to 12 visits). On the HSA plans, office visits and prescriptions apply to the combined deductible before copays/coinsurance begin. Value 1000 and Enhanced 500 office visit and Rx copays are not subject to the deductible.
• Rx copays (all plans): $10 generic/specialty, $40 preferred brand, $80 non-preferred brand. On HSA plans, before the deductible is met each fill costs 3x the copay (applied to the deductible); after the deductible is met, the regular copay applies.
• Two-person and family contracts use family deductible and out-of-pocket amounts applied to total household usage. Rx cost-sharing counts toward the out-of-pocket maximum.
• Preventive care is covered at 100% and is not included.
